# ADR 0029: Initiative, rounds, activations, action points and bounded combat dialogue

- Status: Accepted and implemented in P11.2 (30 October 2026). Attack, defense, damage and the opening strike's resolution (P11.3), reactions and effect timing (P11.4), terminal outcomes, negotiation responses and settlement (P11.5) and checkpoint recovery (P11.6) build on it.
- Date: 30 October 2026
- Builds on: [ADR 0006](0006-original-combat.md) (AI RPG Skirmish), [ADR 0003](0003-canonical-transactions.md) (one canonical commit), [ADR 0026](0026-bounded-world-clock-travel-duration-and-rest.md) (time moves only through commits), [ADR 0028](0028-encounter-lifecycle-frozen-profiles-and-zone-graph.md) (frozen encounters and the lifecycle).

## Context

After P11.1 a player could enter an encounter, but nothing could happen inside it: every exploration intention answered `MODE_ILLEGAL` and no tactical intention existed. The combat design (docs/11 sections 6, 7 and 7.1) fixes the scheduler (persistent initiative with stable tie-breaks, rounds, activations with expiring action points, eight-second rounds) and asks that the player can keep talking during a fight without speech becoming a free source of effects: one bounded speech-only utterance per activation, and consequential social or scene-changing intent only through declared, priced actions submitted at the player's own decision boundary.

## Decision

1. **Initiative is its own operation.** `ENGAGE` still freezes the encounter and draws nothing (ADR 0028). The next intention, `ROLL_INITIATIVE` (no payload, legal only at `ENTERING`), draws two d10 per participant in roster order (ordinals `2i`, `2i+1`), adds each frozen initiative modifier and orders by total descending, finesse descending, participant ID ascending. Totals and ranks are stored once (`encounter_participant.initiative`, `initiative_rank`); a V23 trigger refuses any later change and a first round that opens before every participant is ranked. The roll's receipt lists the dice like any recorded draw, and a retried request replays them.
2. **One pure scheduler.** `CombatScheduler` (in `org.rpgengine.combat`) implements the `skirmish-initiative-rounds/1` kernel. Opening a round increments it and refreshes active participants' reaction budgets once. Activations run over active participants in rank order; defeated, escaped and surrendered participants are skipped. An activation start grants the profile's action points and resets the speech allowance; its end expires unused points. After the last activation the round ends and adds `roundSeconds` of world time. The scheduler never opens a round beyond the round cap and stops at a round end when the player no longer stands; P11.5 decides those encounters.
3. **The schedule stops only at the player's decision.** Every tactical operation advances until the player's own activation waits for an action (`AWAITING_ACTION` with the player at the cursor). Because the player acts once per round, an operation crosses at most one round boundary, so it adds at most one round's seconds. Each encounter row state the operation passes through is written as its own row version, so the V22 lifecycle trigger checks every arrow.
4. **Other participants pass until they have tactics.** Allies and hostiles take real activations (points granted, then expired) but choose no action in P11.2: attacks arrive with P11.3, and constrained tactic choice and the deterministic fallback with P12.3/P12.4. Recording the pass keeps the order, the cursor and the clock honest instead of skipping turns silently.
5. **Five tactical intentions, legal only in encounter mode.** `ROLL_INITIATIVE {}`, `MOVE_ZONE {zoneId}`, `END_ACTIVATION {}`, `COMBAT_SPEAK {recipientId, text ≤ 500}` and `INTERACT {actionId, targetId?}`. The common semantic gate admits them only in `ENCOUNTER` mode and exploration intentions only in `EXPLORATION` mode (`MODE_ILLEGAL`). Except for the roll, each needs the player's decision boundary (`TURN_UNAVAILABLE`), so nothing can be submitted or queued out of turn. Tactical intentions read only the frozen encounter, never the pinned pack, so a fight in progress does not depend on reloading content.
6. **Movement is one edge per action.** `MOVE_ZONE` crosses one unblocked directed edge from the player's zone into a declared zone below its capacity, at `moveCost` (times `difficultTerrainMultiplier` on a difficult edge), paid from the remaining points (`ZONE_UNAVAILABLE`, `ACTION_POINTS_INSUFFICIENT`). The activation stays open at any point total: only `END_ACTIVATION` passes control, because a speech-only utterance is still possible at zero points.
7. **Addressing is legal targeting by voice.** A participant can address a different participant who is active or has surrendered within `maxRangeEdges` range bands (range ignores blocking: a closed gate does not stop a voice). The same predicate gates speech recipients and social-action recipients (`TARGET_ILLEGAL`). Attack targeting (melee reach, weapon range, cover, line of sight) is part of attacks and lands with P11.3.
8. **Speech is bounded and free.** `COMBAT_SPEAK` stores the player's words as a public `COMBAT_SPEECH` event. It costs no action point, draws nothing, passes no world time and does not move the cursor; at most `speechPerActivation` utterances per activation (default 1, `SPEECH_LIMIT_REACHED`). Generated replies and their knowledge scope are P12.4.
9. **Consequential intent needs a declared action.** Encounter templates may declare up to eight `actions`: `social` actions (intent `persuade`, `intimidate`, `negotiate`, `distract` or `request`; recipients `hostile`, `ally` or `any`) and `scene` actions (an optional zone to act from and one passage between two zones joined by a declared edge, set to blocked or open). Each costs its declared points (default `interactCost`, at least 1, at most one activation). `INTERACT` is refused for an undeclared action, from the wrong zone, or when the passage already has the requested state (`INTERACTION_UNAVAILABLE`), so a no-op is never charged. A social attempt is recorded as `INTERACTION_ATTEMPTED` and establishes no outcome: the recipient's response, any social check and agreements are P11.5. A scene action changes the passage in `encounter.passages`, and movement immediately follows the new space.
10. **The database repeats the scheduling guarantees.** V23 adds the cursor (`actor_cursor`, `action_points`, set exactly while an activation is in progress), `speech_used`, `passages`, and per participant `initiative`, `initiative_rank` (unique per encounter) and `reactions`. Its triggers refuse points beyond the frozen allowance, points granted anywhere but an activation start, speech beyond the frozen allowance or growing by more than one per update, a reset of the speech count outside an activation start or a round end, and a re-roll.
11. **The receipt shows the decision.** A tactical receipt carries `combat`: encounter, phase, round, the player's zone, the acting participant and its remaining points, the public initiative order and, at the player's decision, the legal options (affordable moves with cost, speech allowance and addressable recipients, legal declared actions with cost and admissible targets, end activation). Every participant is observable since P11.1, so none of these values is private.

12. **Every entered fight can roll.** Role values stay in the supported range 0–6 (`CombatProfile.ROLE_MIN`/`ROLE_MAX`, docs/11 section 4), so an initiative total always fits its stored 2–32. The pack compiler refuses a role mapped to an attribute declared beyond the range (`CONSTRAINT_CONFLICT`) and `ENGAGE` refuses a stored value outside it or that is not an integer (`ENCOUNTER_UNAVAILABLE`). Before this rule a pack attribute of range 0–100 could enter a fight whose roll then failed its V23 check on every attempt, leaving the campaign in encounter mode at `ENTERING` with no way out.

## Alternatives considered

- Rolling initiative inside the `ENGAGE` commit: rejected; it would break ADR 0028's draw-free entry, and the stored `ENTERING` state is the frozen pre-initiative state the player can review before dice are cast.
- Ending the activation automatically at zero points: rejected; docs/11 makes the end explicit, and speech remains possible at zero points.
- Widening the stored initiative bound to the largest pack attribute (2–220) instead of bounding role values: rejected; template participants and the default formulas assume the supported range, and later Guard and attack calculations would inherit unbalanced values the provisional numbers were never meant for.
- Skipping non-player activations until they have tactics: rejected; the order, reaction refresh and round time would then be wrong for later tasks.
- Treating any chat text as a social action: rejected by docs/11 section 7.1; words change a fight only through declared definitions with costs, and requested outcomes stay attempts.
- A generic "interact" with free-text effects: rejected; scene changes are limited to declared passages until more scene primitives are designed.

## Consequences

- New refusal codes `COMMAND_TURN_UNAVAILABLE`, `COMMAND_ACTION_POINTS_INSUFFICIENT`, `COMMAND_ZONE_UNAVAILABLE`, `COMMAND_SPEECH_LIMIT_REACHED` and `COMMAND_INTERACTION_UNAVAILABLE` (HTTP 422) with reviewed English and Italian wording (the Italian still needs independent human review); `TARGET_ILLEGAL` also covers unaddressable participants.
- Eight public event types (`INITIATIVE_ROLLED`, `ROUND_STARTED`, `ROUND_ENDED`, `ACTIVATION_STARTED`, `ACTIVATION_ENDED`, `PARTICIPANT_MOVED`, `COMBAT_SPEECH`, `INTERACTION_ATTEMPTED`) with English and Italian statements; the player's own decisions are attributed to the player's actor, scheduler bookkeeping to nobody.
- The frozen definition gains `actions` only when a template declares some, so action-free templates keep their P11.1 definition and hash. The backup tool covers schema version 23 (no new table).
- Limits: the opening strike stays `PENDING` and its slot passes without effect until P11.3 resolves and charges it; non-player participants pass; reactions are refreshed but never spent (P11.4); no terminal predicate is evaluated and a capped encounter waits at its round end for P11.5; social attempts have no response yet; the V16 checkpoint still records no encounter (P11.6); no frontend control or panel (P12.1/P12.2).
