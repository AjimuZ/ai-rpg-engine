# ADR 0036: An explicit settlement finalizes the stored verdict once and returns the campaign to exploration

- Status: Accepted; implemented in P11.5-C.3 (13 November 2026). Completes the outcome pending of [ADR 0032](0032-terminal-predicates-from-committed-state.md), [ADR 0033](0033-negotiation-explicit-answers-and-surrender.md) and decision 8 of [ADR 0035](0035-withdraw-and-the-round-cap.md); changes none of their decisions.
- Date: 13 November 2026
- Builds on: [ADR 0003](0003-canonical-transactions.md) (receipts and replay), [ADR 0029](0029-initiative-rounds-activations-and-bounded-combat-dialogue.md) (encounter mode and the player's decisions), [ADR 0031](0031-predeclared-reactions-effect-phases-and-cooldowns.md) (effect clocks), [ADR 0032](0032-terminal-predicates-from-committed-state.md) (verdict from committed state).

## Context

Docs/11 section 14 asks for one settlement per encounter that atomically finalizes the outcome, positions, remaining conditions, time adjustment, casualty and recovery policy, objectives, rewards, quest and faction changes, events and the next pending decision, refills nothing unless a rule says so, publishes a mechanical recap, requests narration, and cannot be repeated by a refresh, reconnect or a new narration session. Section 6 adds that a fight ending inside a round advances that round once to its boundary without a second condition tick, and section 11 that a remaining combat condition converts to exploration by a defined conversion recorded at settlement. Section 16 requires that settlement be idempotent and that every outcome return to a valid campaign mode; docs/20 section 9 that an accepted agreement triggers one settlement and that a lost response returns the same receipt.

After P11.5-C.2 every terminal path stops at `OUTCOME_PENDING`, which ADR 0035 made the pause for review, but nothing leaves it: the campaign stays in encounter mode and refuses every command. The documents leave open: who starts settlement; what it does when no pack declares rewards, a recovery rule, a conversion of combat effects or where an escape leads; and how it is kept to once.

## Decision

1. **The player settles explicitly.** `SETTLE_ENCOUNTER` {encounterId} is a tactical intention, legal only in encounter mode while the named encounter waits at `OUTCOME_PENDING` and its player participant is the acting player. It costs nothing and no effect prevents it. Any other state, a fight still going on or another encounter's identity is `TURN_UNAVAILABLE`. Keeping the pause readable until the player confirms matches ADR 0035 decision 8 and the explicit-decision rule of combat dialogue; settling inside the deciding operation would have removed the review the round cap promises.
2. **The stored fight, not the request, says how it ended.** Settlement calls `EncounterOutcome.decide` on the state stored at `OUTCOME_PENDING`; the request names only the encounter. A stored outcome without a verdict is a broken guarantee and fails the operation rather than inventing an ending.
3. **One encounter, one settlement, kept by the existing guards.** The settlement key is the encounter itself: the plan writes `SETTLED` and then `NARRATIVE_RESUME` as two row versions, which the V22 arrows allow exactly once and never undo, and both phases are inactive, so the active-encounter index releases the campaign. The same operation returns the campaign to exploration with no pending decision. A lost response is retried with the same request ID and replays the stored receipt before any rule runs; a fresh request is refused as `MODE_ILLEGAL`, because the campaign has left encounter mode. No migration or settlement table is needed.
4. **Leaving encounter mode is settlement's alone.** `ResolvedCommit` accepts tactical progress with exploration mode only when its last step is `NARRATIVE_RESUME`, and requires exploration mode then; the canonical commit refuses any plan that takes an encounter-mode campaign to exploration without such progress. This mirrors the P11.1 rule that only an entry enters encounter mode.
5. **An unfinished round runs out once.** When the cursor is still set at `OUTCOME_PENDING`, the fight ended inside a round, and settlement adds that round's `roundSeconds` once (docs/11 section 6); no effect phase runs, so no tick fires for those seconds. A fight decided at a round end has already added its time. The bounded clock must take the seconds (`TIME_ILLEGAL` otherwise).
6. **Combat effects end with the fight; exploration conditions do not change.** No pack declares how a combat effect would carry over, so every effect instance and Guard stance still held ends at settlement, each listed in the recap (`effectsEnded`). The fight never wrote the player's exploration conditions, and settlement does not either: a condition the player brought keeps its exploration row, and an effect applied in the fight does not become one. This is the most conservative conversion that docs/11 section 11 allows: recorded, and changing nothing it cannot justify.
7. **Nothing else moves.** Pools are not refilled: a defeated player stays at the Health pool's minimum, which is the `incapacitated` recovery state; it can wait, travel and rest, and cannot start another fight until its Health rises. Nobody's encounter zone or standing changes, and the player's campaign location stays the encounter's location, also after an escape, since no pack maps an escape zone to a place. Hostiles and allies without a campaign actor have nothing outside the encounter to update. No reward, loot, quest or faction step is settled (challenge-source rewards are P13.4); as after every tactical command, the next exploration command evaluates the quest facts the fight changed.
8. **One public recap, attributed to the player.** `ENCOUNTER_SETTLED` carries the verdict's public form, the round, the seconds added, every participant's final side, zone and standing, and the ended effects; English and Italian statements say the story resumes, the seconds when there are any and that combat effects ended. The receipt's `combat` block shows phase `NARRATIVE_RESUME`, the verdict, the final standings and `settlement` {seconds, effectsEnded}. Like every command, settlement owes narration, which is requested from this committed result; missing narration does not prevent reading it.

## Alternatives considered

- Settling automatically in the operation that reaches the verdict: rejected; it removes the readable pause ADR 0035 relies on, and it would have required rewriting every stored `OUTCOME_PENDING` expectation.
- A settlement row or table with its own unique key: rejected for now; the phase machine already allows the arrow once, the encounter row keeps the agreement and history, and the event stores the recap. A table can come with rewards in P13.4.
- Keeping the fight's effects as exploration conditions, or removing the player's entry conditions: rejected; neither is declared by any pack, so both would invent consequences.
- Moving an escaped player to a neighbouring location: rejected; escape zones are encounter-local and name no place.
- Restoring a defeated player to minimum viable Health: rejected; docs/11 forbids refills that no rule declares.
- A settlement payload naming the outcome: rejected; the request would then be able to choose how the fight ended.

## Consequences

- New intention `SETTLE_ENCOUNTER` {encounterId} and `TacticalIntent.Settle`; new public event `ENCOUNTER_SETTLED` with English and Italian statements; receipt `combat.settlement`. No migration, backup schema change, refusal category or message.
- The campaign accepts exploration commands and a new `ENGAGE` after settlement; the settled encounter stays as history at `NARRATIVE_RESUME`.
- Limits: no reward, loot, recovery rule, effect conversion or escape destination is declarable yet (P13.4, P13); a fight that ended inside a round cannot be settled within one round of the clock's end, because the round would not fit; an encounter stored at the end of its last round before P11.5-C.2 still cannot move, because it never reached `OUTCOME_PENDING`; the operational checkpoint still records no encounter (P11.6); no frontend control or generated outcome narration exists yet (P12).
