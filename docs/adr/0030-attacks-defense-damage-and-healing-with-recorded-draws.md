# ADR 0030: Attacks, defense, damage and healing with recorded draws

- Status: Accepted and implemented in P11.3 (30 October 2026). Reactions and effect timing (P11.4), terminal outcomes and settlement (P11.5), checkpoint recovery (P11.6) and constrained tactics (P12.3/P12.4) build on it.
- Date: 30 October 2026
- Builds on: [ADR 0006](0006-original-combat.md) (AI RPG Skirmish), [ADR 0028](0028-encounter-lifecycle-frozen-profiles-and-zone-graph.md) (frozen encounters), [ADR 0029](0029-initiative-rounds-activations-and-bounded-combat-dialogue.md) (scheduler and tactical intentions).

## Context

After P11.2 a fight ran turn by turn but nobody could hurt anybody: other participants passed, the opening strike stayed pending, and items carried no Guard or armor. Docs/11 sections 8 and 9 fix the arithmetic (`2d10 + attribute + skill + modifier`, meeting the defense succeeds, a margin of six adds `1d4` once, flat armor with penetration, Health never below zero, healing never above the maximum and never reviving), and ask that every roll is recorded with its calculation. Several things those sections leave open had to be decided to make the rules executable: where attack data lives, how targeting reads cover and line of sight, what a charge is, who may see the calculation, and what non-player participants do before P12.

## Decision

1. **The arithmetic is one pure class.** `CombatMath` computes the attack roll, effective Guard, damage and healing from recorded dice and frozen values, with no draw, storage or legality of its own, so a stored calculation can be recomputed and the rules can be tested exhaustively.
2. **Attack data is pack data, frozen at entry.** An equippable item may declare `combat` {`guard`, `armor`, `attack`}; a template participant may declare `attacks`. At `ENGAGE` the worn items' Guard modifiers (capped together by `equipmentGuardCap`), armor (capped by `armorMax`) and weapons become the player's frozen profile, each weapon with the player's rank in its skill. A later content revision or equipment change cannot alter a fight in progress. A frozen form without `attacks`, written before this task, reads back as a participant that cannot attack. Ruleset roles (`force`, `finesse`, `insight`, `resolve`) name the attributes, because packs define their own vocabularies.
3. **Strike has a fixed draw order.** `STRIKE {attackId, targetId}` costs `strikeCost`. It draws two d10, then on a hit each damage die, then on a strong hit the strong-hit die, so a miss reserves no damage draw and a retry replays the same values. Action points and ammunition are paid by the attempt, hit or miss.
4. **Targeting is judged at the moment of the attack.** A target must be a standing participant of the opposing side. Melee (`range` 0) needs the same zone; a ranged attack reaches its `range` in zone distance, which ignores blocking as P11.1 decided, so a closed gate does not stop a shot. From another zone an obscured zone hides its occupants (no line of sight), full cover stops the attack and partial cover adds `partialCoverBonus` to Guard. Inside one zone neither cover nor visibility applies: nothing stands between the two. Allies cannot be attacked.
5. **One Guard stance.** `GUARD` costs `guardCost`, once per activation, and adds `guardBonus` to Guard until the start of the participant's next activation (`encounter_participant.guarding`). Effective Guard is the frozen Guard plus stance plus cover, the situational part capped by `situationalGuardCap`. Brace (P11.4) joins the same stacking key by taking the higher bonus, not the sum. Resistance attacks read Resistance alone.
6. **A charge is a unit of ammunition.** An attack may name an item consumed per attempt. There is no separate charge counter on an instance; a device with charges is modelled as a device and a stack of its charges, which the existing inventory ledger already conserves. Only unworn stacks can be spent, in identity order.
7. **The record has two halves.** Each strike writes a public `STRIKE_RESOLVED` event (dice, score, whether it hit, whether it was strong, damage taken, and for a player target its own defense and Health) and an internal `STRIKE_CALCULATED` event with the complete calculation and draw ordinals. The target's defense, armor and Health stay out of the public event and the receipt when they belong to another participant, following the exploration rule that other actors' pools are hidden; what a player can infer from recorded numbers is real observation. The draws themselves stay in `operation_random_draw` with algorithm, version, operation and ordinal.
8. **Healing is an effect and an action.** The `heal` effect restores the Health pool the pack's combat rule names, at most the missing Health, and records `NOT_REVIVED` for a defeated actor. It now runs in exploration too and needs a combat rule in the player's vocabulary (`EFFECT_UNSUPPORTED` otherwise). `COMBAT_USE_ITEM {itemInstanceId, targetId?}` runs a pinned use contract in a fight for `useItemCost`: self-policy on oneself, single-target on the player or on a standing ally in the player's zone, whose encounter-local Health only `heal` can reach. It is the one tactical intention that reads the pinned catalog, because a use contract is pack data; other tactical intentions keep reading only the frozen encounter. Other target policies wait for effect targeting (P11.4). `modifyResource` keeps its exploration meaning, so an item can also fell its own user, who is then defeated while the schedule continues.
9. **Zero Health defeats, never kills.** A participant at zero is `DEFEATED`, takes no activation, holds no stance and cannot be targeted. Without terminal predicates the fight then continues (or waits at a round end when the player fell) until P11.5 decides it.
10. **Opening strikes resolve once, in the initiative operation.** Under `first` the strike resolves right after round one opens, and the player's round-one grant is reduced by its cost, because points may only be granted at an activation start. Under `initiative` it resolves when the player's round-one activation starts. Legality is judged at the slot: a strike that cannot be made lapses with a public reason (`OPENING_STRIKE_LAPSED`), spending and drawing nothing, including when the player fell before its slot. The attack is the first frozen attack that can reach the target.
11. **A small interim tactic.** Non-player participants need to act for attacks to mean anything, but constrained choices and the full fallback are P12.3/P12.4. Until then a participant without an attack passes, as it did; an armed one moves one edge toward the nearest opponent when none is in reach, makes at most one strike at the nearest opponent in reach (then roster order) and takes the Guard action with what is left. It goes through the same legality as the player's intentions.
12. **The bounds are proven per operation.** At most four damage dice per attack and one non-player strike per activation keep an operation at 81 draws at worst (18 initiative dice, one opening strike, eight strikes of seven), under the durable budget of 128; an item contract used in a fight may draw at most 64, so the schedule that continues if it fells the player still fits. `TacticalPlan.MAX_STEPS` rises from 64 to 128, because an activation now writes up to nine row versions.
13. **The database repeats the Health guarantees.** V24 adds `guarding` and a trigger: local Health stays within the frozen maximum, zero Health cannot stay `ACTIVE`, Health cannot rise from zero and a defeated participant cannot return. The player's Health stays in `actor_resource`, whose own bounds apply. No new table is needed: the calculation is in events and draws.

## Alternatives considered

- Hard-coding the sample weapons: rejected; docs/11 makes equipment original pack data, and the pack contract already rejects unknown fields, so a closed `combat` object extends it safely.
- Reading attack data from the pinned pack during the fight: rejected; ADR 0028 freezes everything an encounter depends on at entry.
- One public event with the whole calculation: rejected; it would disclose hidden defenses and Health and pre-empt P12.1's "known defenses".
- A charge counter on item instances: rejected for now; it needs a new mutable item fact and a migration, while a stack of charges reuses the conserved ledger.
- Drawing damage dice before knowing the hit: rejected; it would spend draw budget on nothing and gains no fairness, since draws are unpredictable and bound to the operation either way.
- Leaving non-player participants passive until P12: rejected; attacks, Guard, defeat of the player and the worked fight could not then be exercised end to end.
- Automatic end of the activation when no affordable action remains: rejected as in ADR 0029; speech can remain possible and the end stays explicit.

## Consequences

- New tactical intentions `STRIKE`, `GUARD` and `COMBAT_USE_ITEM`; new refusals `COMMAND_ATTACK_UNAVAILABLE` and `COMMAND_GUARD_UNAVAILABLE` (HTTP 422) with reviewed English and Italian wording (the Italian still needs independent human review); `TARGET_ILLEGAL` also covers unreachable attack targets and ineligible item targets.
- Receipts gain `combat.participants` (public standing), `combat.strikes` (public outcomes) and the options `strikes` (with costs, targets and a stable `disabled` reason), `guard` and `useItem`.
- A fight can now hurt the player, and the player can fall at a round end with no decision left. Until P11.5 such a campaign stays in encounter mode; that is a documented limit, not a new dead end for finished content, because no shipped adventure declares encounters yet.
- The worked fight of docs/20 section 3 plays from its recorded dice except for Brace, which P11.4 adds; there the opponent's 13 meets an unbraced Guard 12.
- An encounter that rolled initiative before this release with a strike still pending resolves it at the player's next activation, once.
- Balance remains provisional (P18.3): nothing here claims a fight is fair, and condition modifiers and absorption stay at zero until P11.4 supplies them, although the formulas already consume them.
