# Visual-Novel Story UI — Prompt and Provenance

Date: 23 October 2026. Project: AI RPG Engine.

Created with the built-in ImageGen tool as a new image without reference images.
The selected [visual-novel-ui-v1.png](visual-novel-ui-v1.png) was visually
inspected and copied unchanged into the project. Main labels, attributed
speaker text, composer and suggestion chips are legible. The harbor, Mira and
the sample exchange are illustrative content, not required lore or an action
chosen by a real player. Italian text demonstrates localization.

This flattened concept is documentation, not implemented software or the
separate production graphics required by P05.5. No external artwork, music or

## Final prompt

```text
Use case: ui-mockup. Create one polished high-fidelity concept screenshot of a dynamic visual-novel RPG interface, landscape 16:9, all edges visible, no monitor or browser frame. This is the principal story screen of AI RPG Engine. Prioritize a huge immersive central illustration and a classic visual-novel dialogue box, with minimal chrome and generous readable text. Original cinematic hand-painted anime/fantasy environment: a misty cliffside harbor at blue twilight, glowing warm lanterns, a distant dark lighthouse, sea and layered rooftops; an adult navigator woman in a practical teal coat stands at the left side of the scene, waist-up, expressive but natural. Scene artwork occupies about the upper two-thirds and virtually the full width. Palette: deep navy/obsidian, muted violet, warm lantern gold, restrained cyan accents. Refined clean UI typography, subtle borders, excellent contrast. Compact top bar: 'AI RPG Engine' left; 'La costa delle lanterne' near the center; 'Salvato' at right. Small unobtrusive navigation labels 'Storia', 'Personaggio', 'Diario', 'Mappa', 'Audio', 'Crediti'. Do not show inventory grids, equipment sidebars, model settings, token counters or combat controls. Below the illustration a broad dark translucent dialogue panel, speaker nameplate 'MIRA', small matching speaker portrait, exact large Italian dialogue: 'Il faro si è spento. Vuoi che controlliamo il sentiero?' The speaker name and portrait are unmistakable. A discreet compact earlier-exchange strip above or within the panel shows two clearly subordinate lines: 'Narratore: La nebbia avvolge il porto.' and 'Tu: Aspetta, sento dei passi.' Keep these visibly separate from Mira's current dialogue. Beneath the current dialogue include a quiet 'Continua' control for reading the next already available passage and a 'Cronologia' control. Then a persistent player composer along the bottom with placeholder 'Scrivi cosa dici o fai…' and a clear 'Invia' button. Above the composer, two restrained suggestion chips 'Chiedi del faro' and 'Osserva il sentiero', plus small label 'Suggerimenti'. The screenshot should look like a coherent usable visual novel, with the scene dominant, dynamic character/narrator/player text, and clear player agency. The story, location and character are illustrative sample content, not mandatory lore. Crisp readable main labels, no watermarks, no existing franchise characters, no multi-screen collage, no annotations outside the interface.
```
